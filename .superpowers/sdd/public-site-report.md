# Public site redesign: report (2026-10-22, branch feat/friends)

## What changed
- New public site in `apps/web/src/site/` replacing `routes/Landing.tsx` + `routes/landing/*` (removed).
  Velvet by default; every page follows the visitor's saved look (tokens only).
- Homepage (Persuade): "Your people, in colour." hero with one primary path (Create your account → /signup)
  plus Open web app (/messages) and Sign in in the header; a live product moment above the fold (a group
  chat in a phone whose look the visitor switches: Velvet / Aurora / Bloom, a keyboard radio group);
  Friends by username; group colours; photos/clips/voice + disappearing; honest privacy section
  (server can/can't see, "not yet independently audited"); FAQ; closing band.
- Shared header (Features, Privacy, FAQ, Download, Sign in, Create account; no-JS `<details>` menu on
  phones) and footer (site + app links, audit note, full-width wordmark).
- One authored motion per page: hero look switch (colour ease + thread re-settles, only after a switch);
  privacy page packet glide. Both are stopped by the global reduced-motion rules. Lenis smooth scroll and
  the `motion` library are no longer used on public pages.
- Existing claims kept (six features, server lists, five FAQ answers, closing line, audit note). One
  edited for truth: "works … on iOS and Android" now says the phone app isn't in the stores yet.

## Pages
`/`, `/features`, `/privacy-and-security`, `/faq`, `/download` (web available now; phone app exists but
is not in the App Store / Google Play; no store links, no "coming soon").

## SEO artifacts
- `src/site/meta.ts`: single source for titles, descriptions, canonical, OG/Twitter, JSON-LD
  (Organization + SoftwareApplication with name/url/category/OS/description only; FAQPage on /faq),
  sitemap and robots.
- `public/og.png` 1200×630 generated by `scripts/og-image.mjs` (Playwright + self-hosted fonts, all
  requests blocked).
- `dist/sitemap.xml` (5 public URLs), `dist/robots.txt` (disallows /messages, /settings, /auth/,
  /reset-password; /friends, /login, /signup stay fetchable so link-preview bots show the generic card).
- App routes (incl. `/friends?add=…`) are served from `dist/app.html`: noindex, generic card, no user data;
  `vercel.json` rewrites + `X-Robots-Tag` on app.html. Client also resets head to noindex when the app starts.

## Prerender approach
`scripts/prerender.mjs` runs after `vite build` (in `pnpm build`): Vite SSR-builds
`src/site/entry-server.tsx`, `renderToString` per page into the built index.html with that page's head
tags, Velvet font preloads and modulepreloads from the Vite manifest (manifest is then deleted). The client
(`src/site/client.tsx`) preloads the page chunk and hydrates; verified no hydration errors in a prod build.
No new dependency. `vite preview` mimics vercel.json via a small plugin.

## Concerns
- Canonical/sitemap origin falls back to `https://vybora.app` (the domain the mobile app uses) because
  VITE_SITE_URL is localhost; confirm the real domain.
- Mobile headings run to three lines at 390 px on some pages (readable, but over the taste skill's 2-line rule).
- Hero/collage imagery is product UI and a drawn lake "photo"; no real photography exists or was fetched.
- `lenis` is now unused in apps/web but left in package.json to avoid a lockfile change offline.
- Signed-in visitors on `/` briefly see the prerendered homepage before the app loads and redirects.
- The Vercel rewrite/folder behaviour is configured but not deployed/verified.

## Owner actions
1. Set `VITE_SITE_URL` to the production origin in the hosting env (not .env) and rebuild.
2. Deploy and check `/features` etc. serve their prerendered HTML and `/friends?add=x` serves app.html.
3. Submit sitemap.xml in Search Console; validate the card with a social preview debugger.
4. Optionally `pnpm --filter @vybora/web remove lenis`; re-run `node apps/web/scripts/og-image.mjs` after brand changes.

## Fix round 1
1. Signed-in flash on `/`: inline `#app-gate` script in index.html (same `sb-*-auth-token` test as main.tsx)
   sets `data-app` so `#root` is hidden until `startApp` mounts and removes it. Signed-out visitors are
   never hidden (verified in a prod preview with JS blocked: signed-in hidden, signed-out visible). Tested.
2. Soft 404s: vercel.json now rewrites only the app prefixes (/messages, /friends, /settings, /login,
   /signup, /forgot-password, /reset-password, /auth/callback) to app.html; the prerender also writes
   `dist/404.html` (noindex app shell), so unknown paths get a real 404 and the app's NotFound
   ("This page isn't here", robots noindex, verified in preview). `X-Robots-Tag` on app.html and 404.html.
3. Footer year is a build-time constant (`__BUILD_YEAR__` via Vite `define`).
4. Prerender throws on missing Velvet fonts and on page sources missing from the manifest; `.prerender/`
   is removed in a `finally`.
5. Rewrite exclusion list removed: only the app prefixes are rewritten (so files never are).
6. Phone preview timestamps and the "End-to-end encrypted" caption: 10 px subtle → 11 px muted ink;
   outgoing timestamp at full opacity; photo caption scrim 45% → 70%. Server-sees list text is already
   16 px body / muted ink (its icons are decorative).
7. The build prints a loud warning when VITE_SITE_URL is unset or local (it is, here).
